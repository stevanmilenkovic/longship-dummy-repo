# 5 Reasons Your Email Marketing Strategy Is Failing (And How to Fix It)

Email marketing remains one of the most cost-effective channels available to modern businesses. With an average ROI of 36x, it consistently outperforms paid social and display advertising. For marketing teams under pressure to demonstrate measurable returns, email deserves serious strategic investment.

Yet despite this potential, a significant number of campaigns underperform. The reasons are often structural rather than creative — and they are fixable.

## 1. You Are Not Segmenting Your Audience

Sending the same message to your entire list is one of the most common and damaging mistakes in email marketing. Subscribers have different needs, different levels of familiarity with your brand, and different points in the buying journey. A first-time visitor requires a different message than a loyal customer who has purchased three times.

Effective segmentation does not need to be complex. Begin with two or three core segments — new subscribers, active buyers, and lapsed contacts — and tailor your messaging accordingly. Even this basic structure will improve relevance and reduce unsubscribe rates.

## 2. Your Subject Lines Are Not Doing Their Job

Research from Campaign Monitor (2023) indicates that 47% of recipients decide whether to open an email based on the subject line alone. Subject lines should be specific, reference the subscriber's industry, role, or prior behaviour to signal the email is meant for them specifically, and give the reader a reason to act now rather than later.

Avoid vague teasers and clickbait. Instead, lead with the value: what will the reader learn or gain by opening this email? Test two or three variations per campaign and let performance data guide your approach over time.

## 3. You Are Ignoring Your Sending Frequency

Sending cadence is one of the most overlooked drivers of list health and engagement. Too many emails and subscribers tune out or unsubscribe. Too few and they forget who you are. The right cadence depends on your audience, your content volume, and your campaign goals.

A practical starting point is one to two emails per week for active campaigns, with a lower frequency for nurture sequences. Monitor unsubscribe rates and engagement trends closely. If unsubscribe rates rise above 0.5% or open rates fall more than 10% week-over-week, adjust your cadence before the damage compounds.

## 4. Your Emails Are Not Optimised for Mobile

According to Litmus's 2023 State of Email report, more than half of all emails are opened on mobile devices. Despite this, many marketing teams still design primarily for desktop and treat mobile as an afterthought. The result is emails that are difficult to read, with text that is too small, buttons that are hard to tap, and images that fail to load correctly.

Mobile optimisation is not optional. Use single-column layouts, minimum 16px body text, and touch-friendly CTAs with adequate padding. Test every send across multiple devices before deployment.

## 5. You Are Not Acting on Your Data

Email platforms surface a rich set of performance metrics that directly inform strategic decisions. Open rates, click-through rates, conversion data, and unsubscribe trends all tell a story about what is working and what is not. Without acting on this data, teams are likely to repeat the same mistakes and miss clear opportunities for improvement.

Set aside time after each campaign to review performance against your benchmarks. Identify the one or two things that worked well and the one thing to change next time. Apply those learnings to the next send. Consistently applied, this feedback loop drives measurable improvement in open rates, click-through rates, and conversions.
